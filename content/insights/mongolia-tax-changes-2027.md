On 26 June 2026 the State Great Khural adopted amendments to the package of tax laws. Most of them take effect on 1 January 2027.

One point is worth naming correctly at the outset. A wholly new "Tax Act" does not come into force in 2027. What happens is that substantial amendments to several laws — the Corporate Income Tax Law, the Value Added Tax Law, the Personal Income Tax Law and the General Tax Law — take effect at the same time.

## The compulsory VAT registration threshold rises to MNT 400 million

One of the changes with the most direct effect on business is the VAT registration threshold.

The current threshold of MNT 50 million rises to MNT 400 million on 1 January 2027. A person whose sales income as defined in the law reaches MNT 400 million is required to register as a VAT withholding taxpayer.

This does not mean that every business currently registered for VAT with annual turnover below MNT 400 million will be deregistered automatically on 1 January 2027. Deregistration under the law is subject to conditions, including evidence from twelve consecutive months of returns that there was no taxable income or that the registration threshold was not reached.

## Corporate income tax moves to three bands

From 2027 the following bands apply to certain categories of taxable corporate income:

| Annual taxable income | Corporate income tax |
| --- | --- |
| MNT 0–6 billion | 10% |
| MNT 6–10 billion | MNT 600 million + 15% of the amount above MNT 6 billion |
| Above MNT 10 billion | MNT 1.2 billion + 25% of the amount above MNT 10 billion |

These bands apply from 1 January 2027.

It matters not to read MNT 6 billion and MNT 10 billion as total company turnover. Article 20.1 applies the rates above to the "taxable income for the year" as determined under articles 18.2 to 18.5.

Nor does exceeding MNT 6 billion mean 15 per cent on all income, or exceeding MNT 10 billion mean 25 per cent on all income. Under the banding, the higher rate applies only to the amount above the relevant threshold.

## The threshold for 90 per cent tax relief rises to MNT 2.5 billion

For a company established in Mongolia that meets the statutory requirements and operates outside certain sectors, the taxable income threshold for the 90 per cent tax relief rises from MNT 1.5 billion to MNT 2.5 billion.

This change also takes effect on 1 January 2027.

It does not follow that every company with income below MNT 2.5 billion automatically receives the 90 per cent relief. The types of activity and other requirements set out in article 22.1, and the rules aggregating the income of related parties, need to be checked as well.

## Depreciation of certain technology equipment over three years

One change relevant to the information technology sector sets the useful life of servers, equipment for storing and processing data, and computing devices with graphics or special-purpose processors at three years for tax purposes. This applies from 2027.

It is significant for the tax calculations of technology, data processing and artificial intelligence companies that invest in servers and high-performance computing equipment.

## Transitional personal income tax bands apply during 2027

The personal income tax rates change substantially as well. From 1 January to 31 December 2027 the following transitional bands apply:

| Annual taxable income | Tax payable in 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| MNT 0–9,504,000 | 0% |
| MNT 9,504,000–120 million | 10% of the amount above MNT 9,504,000 |
| MNT 120–180 million | MNT 11,049,600 + 15% of the amount above MNT 120 million |
| Above MNT 180 million | MNT 20,049,600 + 20% of the amount above MNT 180 million |

So in 2027, annual taxable income up to MNT 9,504,000 is taxed at zero per cent. And having income above MNT 120 million does not mean 15 per cent applies to all of it — the higher rate applies only to the amount above the threshold of that band.

This is a transitional arrangement for 2027. A different set of bands is separately provided for from 1 January 2028.

## Relief on the sale of a first owner-occupied dwelling

From 2027, income earned by a Mongolian citizen from the sale of a dwelling they owned and lived in for the first time falls within a new personal income tax relief.

Dwellings built in a state tourism zone or in a specially protected area are excluded from the relief under the law.

It is therefore not the case that all income from selling a first dwelling is tax free. Whether the dwelling was genuinely the first one owned and occupied, and whether the other statutory conditions are met, both need checking.

## VAT payment may be deferred on certain conditions

From 2027, a VAT withholding taxpayer whose tax compliance rating is "good" or above may apply to defer payment of VAT by up to one month.

Where the first deferred amount has been paid in full and the good rating has been maintained, a further application may be made to defer payment for up to two months, paid in equal monthly instalments. A similar option is available for import VAT.

If the deferred tax is not paid in full, the compliance rating falls, or it is established that an electronic payment receipt was not issued, the deferral is revoked and collection of the tax debt begins.

## What businesses should check before 2027

Companies preparing their year-end tax planning for 2026 need to factor in the 2027 changes in advance. In particular: how the MNT 400 million VAT threshold affects the company, which of the new corporate income tax bands applies, whether the conditions for the 90 per cent relief are met, and what has changed in the reporting deadlines.

For individuals, the changes to tax on salary, business income and the sale of a dwelling should be worked through by category of income.

## Conclusion

The 2027 tax changes are not simply a matter of rates going up or down. The VAT registration threshold, the corporate income tax bands, relief for small and medium businesses, personal income tax and the option to defer VAT all take effect at once, and each of them bears directly on a taxpayer's day-to-day operations.

The position for 2027 is therefore better worked out during year-end financial and tax planning for 2026 than after 2027 has begun.
