Amendments adopted on 3 July 2026 introduced a more detailed notification-based regulatory regime, and the legislation is now titled the Law on Permits and Notifications.

The central idea is that not every business activity needs prior state approval. The form of oversight now depends on the level of risk.

## Three levels of regulation

High-risk activities are generally subject to special permits, medium-risk activities to ordinary permits, and certain lower-risk activities that still require ongoing supervision may be conducted through notification.

Activities that are neither prohibited nor included in the statutory lists of special permits, ordinary permits or notifications may generally be conducted freely.

## A notification is not an application for approval

This is the most important feature of the notification regime.

A person or entity must register the notification electronically in the unified permit and notification system at least five business days before commencing the activity. Once submitted, the notification is treated as having been delivered to the competent authority.

The law is explicit on one point: a person that has submitted a notification is treated as registered for that activity, and the competent authority is prohibited from issuing a decision approving or rejecting the notification.

A notification is therefore not a simplified licence. It is a different regulatory mechanism, under which the required information is filed in advance and the activity may then begin.

## Which activities fall within the notification regime

The current list includes, among others:

- taxi services;
- professional tax advisory services;
- filming in Mongolia by foreign individuals or legal entities;
- certain procedures relating to a change of name or location of a non-bank financial institution.

## Permit terms and electronic documents

Unless sector-specific legislation provides otherwise, special permits are to be issued for at least ten years and ordinary permits for at least five years.

Permit certificates and equivalent documents are issued electronically, and the electronic document has the same legal effect as the decision granting the permit and the official reference.

## What a business should check

The starting point is to classify the activity correctly against the current lists in articles 8.1, 8.2 and 8.3 — not by reference to what previously required a special permit.

Falling within the notification regime does not mean that the professional, technical and other substantive requirements for the activity disappear. What changes is the form of state oversight applying at the point the activity begins.

Where the information registered in a notification changes, the change must be registered in the unified system. Ceasing the activity requires at least five business days' notice.

## Conclusion

The 2026 reform moves the right to do business away from a model in which the state approves first and the business operates afterwards, towards a clearer separation between permits, notifications and activities that may be conducted freely.
