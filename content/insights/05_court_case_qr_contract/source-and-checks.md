# Source and editorial checks

**Primary source:** Хэнтий аймаг дахь Захиргааны хэргийн анхан шатны шүүх, 2025.04.28, №127/ШШ/2025/0004.

- Court decision: https://shuukh.mn/single_case/18492?bb=1&court_cat=3&id=1
- Case index in the decision: 127/2025/0002/з (the text also displays 127/2025/0002/3).
- Disputed administrative decision: Дархан сумын Засаг даргын 2025.01.20-ны А/06 дугаар захирамж.
- Main statutory references in judgment: Газрын тухай хуулийн 27.1, 34.1, 40.1.6, and the amendment to 23.4.2 referenced by the court.

## Key distinctions checked against the judgment

1. The court found that the landholder's explanations did **not** amount to sufficient justification for non-use.
2. The court nevertheless annulled the administrative order because the underlying land possession agreement's formation and date were not proved; accordingly the two consecutive years could not be established.
3. Contract versions supplied by the parties were dated 2022-12-13 and 2025-01-20; the cadastral system showed the terms as not accepted.
4. The 2023 legislative change concerned the specific land-agreement electronic procedure and cannot be treated as a blanket ban on all electronic contracts before 2023.
5. Whether the first-instance decision was appealed or altered is not confirmed. No finality claim is made.

Only the above judgment was used as a source for the reported litigation facts. Broader contracting-system design suggestions are clearly presented as independent practical analysis, not holdings of the court.
