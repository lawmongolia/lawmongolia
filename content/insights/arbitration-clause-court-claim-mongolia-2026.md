A contract stating that disputes “shall be resolved by arbitration” does not make it physically impossible for one party to file a claim before a state court. However, if there is a **valid and workable arbitration agreement**, and the other party requests arbitration at the procedural stage prescribed by law, the court will generally terminate the court proceedings unless it finds that the arbitration agreement is invalid or incapable of being performed. This consequence is expressly regulated by [Article 10.1 of Mongolia's Arbitration Law](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=12456). An arbitration clause therefore determines more than a preferred forum: it can directly affect how and where contractual claims must be pursued.

## What is an arbitration agreement?

Article 8.1 defines an arbitration agreement as an agreement by the parties to refer all or specified disputes arising or potentially arising in connection with a defined legal relationship to arbitration. Under Article 8.2, the agreement may form part of the main contract or be a separate contract. The law does not require every business arbitration agreement to be contained in a standalone document.

Arbitration and state-court litigation are distinct procedures. The parties can agree to arbitrate the disputes covered by their agreement, but they cannot simply submit every category of dispute to arbitration. Article 9.1 expressly preserves disputes that legislation assigns exclusively to the courts.

## Must the arbitration agreement be a separately signed paper document?

Article **8.3** requires an arbitration agreement to be in writing. Articles **8.4–8.7**, however, recognise the documentation of its content and certain forms of accessible electronic communication as satisfying the written-form requirement. Depending on the evidence, exchanges of emails, an electronic contract or the incorporation of another document containing an arbitration clause can therefore be relevant.

Article **8.9** specifically addresses a document containing an arbitration clause that is referred to in the main contract and expressly made part of that contract. This matters where an arbitration clause is located in a supplier's “General Conditions” or an annex rather than on the signed signature page. The question is whether the parties actually incorporated the relevant terms into their agreement, not simply whether an arbitration clause appears somewhere in the supplier's standard documentation.

## Does cancelling the main contract also cancel its arbitration clause?

Under Article **8.10**, the arbitration agreement forming part of a contract remains effective regardless of issues such as the validity of the main agreement, withdrawal from it by a party or impossibility of contractual performance. Challenging the underlying contract does not, solely for that reason, eliminate the arbitration clause.

Consider an illustrative dispute in which a Mongolian purchaser withdraws from an equipment supply agreement with a foreign supplier because of an alleged contractual breach. The buyer then seeks repayment. Before starting proceedings, the parties must determine whether the repayment dispute falls within their arbitration agreement. The potential invalidity or unworkability of the arbitration agreement itself is a separate inquiry from the validity or termination of the supply contract.

## What happens if a claim is filed in court despite the clause?

Article **10.1** addresses this situation directly. If a claim is filed before a state court despite an arbitration agreement, and a party requests referral to arbitration **before presenting its response**, the court will terminate the court proceedings unless it finds the agreement invalid or incapable of being performed. A party's timely request, the arbitration agreement's validity and the scope of the dispute are therefore central. Starting court proceedings does not automatically supersede the arbitration agreement.

Article **10.2** also allows the arbitration to be commenced or continued, and an award to be made, while court proceedings are under way. Before pursuing the same dispute in more than one forum, the parties should examine jurisdiction, the terms of the arbitration agreement, the procedural position taken by the opponent and any applicable limitation period. Selecting the wrong route can lead to avoidable costs and deadlines being missed. A separate article explains [limitation periods and acknowledgments of contractual debt](/en/insights/contractual-limitation-period-and-acknowledgment-of-debt-mongolia).

## Can a business put a mandatory arbitration clause in standard consumer terms?

Article **8.11** contains an important consumer-protection exception. For a **consumer-rights dispute**, an arbitration agreement may be concluded **only after the dispute has arisen**, as a **separate written agreement**, and must identify the jurisdiction of the arbitration proceedings. A provision routinely included in a business-to-business contract therefore cannot simply be copied into consumer-app terms and assumed to require arbitration of every future consumer dispute.

This distinction is especially relevant to electronic commerce, consumer financial services and standard-form terms in applications and digital platforms. Before assessing an arbitration clause's effect, identify whether the dispute is a business-to-business commercial matter or one involving consumer rights.

## How should an international commercial arbitration clause be drafted?

In a commercial agreement, parties should distinguish the arbitration institution or rules, the place of arbitration, the language, the number of arbitrators and the substantive law governing the dispute. These are not interchangeable decisions. Article 32.1 allows the parties to agree on the place of arbitration, Article 34.1 on the language, and Article 40.1 on the applicable legal rules. Saying that “Mongolian law governs the contract” does not, by itself, establish that the arbitration proceedings take place in Mongolia.

A clause referring vaguely to “arbitration” may leave open disputes about which institution or rules apply, the language of proceedings and the appointment of arbitrators. Although statutory provisions may fill some gaps, agreeing the details at the contracting stage reduces uncertainty. Article 41 identifies expenses such as arbitrators' fees and, for institutional arbitration, the relevant institution's charges. Cost analysis should therefore go beyond simply assuming arbitration will be faster than litigation.

## Does an arbitration clause prevent any assistance from a court?

An agreement to arbitrate does not remove every role for the courts. Under **Article 11.1**, requesting court-ordered measures to preserve or secure the effectiveness of an eventual arbitral award before or during the arbitration does not itself breach the arbitration agreement. Whether a measure is available, and which court has jurisdiction, must still be assessed under the relevant procedural provisions.

When a dispute arises, the starting point is to assemble the executed contract, annexes, general terms, correspondence incorporating the arbitration clause, evidence of the agreement's formation and the documents proving performance or breach. An arbitration clause should never be dismissed as merely standard wording at the end of a contract. It may determine the forum for the entire dispute and substantially affect the parties' procedural options.
