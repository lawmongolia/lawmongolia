Employees in Mongolia are entitled to annual paid leave for each working year. The right to take annual leave generally arises after the employee has worked for six months under the employment agreement.

## Entitlement

The statutory basic annual leave entitlement is 15 working days. Employees with disabilities and employees under the age of 18 are entitled to 20 working days of basic annual leave.

Additional leave is available depending on length of service and whether the employee works under normal or abnormal working conditions.

Annual leave is calculated by reference to the employee's working year, which begins on the date the employment relationship commenced with that employer — not by calendar year.

## Taking leave in parts

An employee may take annual leave in separate periods within the same working year. However, at least one uninterrupted portion must be no shorter than 10 working days.

## Scheduling

The employer must organise the annual leave schedule based on employees' views while also taking account of the normal operation of the business. Under the applicable regulation, the schedule is to be approved during the first quarter of each year.

## Leave pay

Annual leave pay is calculated using the employee's average salary for the relevant working year and is to be paid before the employee takes the leave.

Where employment ends, the employer must calculate the annual leave attributable to the period actually worked and settle the corresponding annual leave pay.

## Conclusion

Annual leave is not a discretionary benefit offered by an employer. It is a statutory employment right that must be calculated and administered in accordance with the Labour Law and the applicable annual leave regulation.
