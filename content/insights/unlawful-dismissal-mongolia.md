An employer may not terminate an employment relationship simply for any reason it chooses. The Labour Law of Mongolia specifies the grounds on which an employer may initiate termination and prohibits termination on grounds not provided by law.

Permitted grounds include liquidation of the employer, redundancy or elimination of a position, proven lack of professional competence or performance, inability to work for medical reasons, repeated disciplinary breaches, and a serious breach expressly identified in the employment agreement as grounds for immediate termination.

## A valid ground alone may not be enough

The employer must also follow the applicable procedure. For certain grounds, including redundancy, poor performance and medical incapacity, the employee must generally receive at least 30 days' prior written or electronic notice.

Where dismissal is imposed as a disciplinary sanction, the employer must notify the employee, obtain an explanation and consider the nature and consequences of the breach before imposing the sanction.

A termination decision must also be issued in writing or electronically, presented to the employee, and a copy provided to the employee.

## Challenging a dismissal

If an employee believes a termination decision is unjustified, the Labour Law provides a 30-day period from receipt of the decision to initiate the applicable labour dispute procedure. The general period for other labour rights disputes is 90 days, so the shorter period for dismissal matters is easy to miss.

## Compensation on reinstatement

Where an employee is reinstated after a competent authority determines that the employment relationship was terminated without proper grounds, the employer must compensate the employee in an amount equal to the employee's previous average salary for the period until the employee is returned to the former position.

## Conclusion

The legality of a dismissal depends not only on the reason stated in the dismissal order, but on whether that reason is supported by evidence and whether the required procedure was followed.
